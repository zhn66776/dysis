# Provider icon sources

These files are unmodified SVG downloads used only to identify the provider
that owns a conversation. The marks remain the property of their owners. They
do not imply sponsorship or endorsement of AgentDesk.

| File | Source | Original owner / terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `claude.svg` | [Claude AI symbol on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Claude_AI_symbol.svg), retrieved from `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Claude_AI_symbol.svg` on 2026-10-29 | Source is identified there as `https://www.anthropic.com`; the file is marked CC0 1.0. Anthropic's [trademark guidelines](https://www.anthropic.com/legal) still apply. |
| `codex.svg` | `https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip`, member `OpenAI-logos(new)/SVGs/OpenAI-black-monoblossom.svg`, retrieved on 2026-10-29 | OpenAI's official Blossom asset. Follow [OpenAI Design Guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) and Marks usage terms. |
| `codex-dark.svg` | Same official OpenAI package, member `OpenAI-logos(new)/SVGs/OpenAI-white-monoblossom.svg` | White Blossom variant for dark surfaces; same OpenAI brand terms. |

`codex.svg` is the black Blossom for light surfaces and `codex-dark.svg` is the
white Blossom for dark surfaces. `claude.svg` is the standalone orange Claude
starburst, rather than the wordmark, so it remains legible at avatar size.

The SVGs were checked for scripts, event-handler attributes, external links,
raster data URLs, and local filesystem references. None were present. Their
viewBoxes, paths, and colors were preserved exactly.
