# Grok and Gemini provider icons

These files are used only to identify the provider of a conversation in the
private AgentDesk UI. They are not used as AgentDesk branding and do not imply
endorsement.

## Grok

- `grok.svg` and `grok-dark.svg` are the same monochrome Grok (xAI) symbol,
  sourced from the maintained theSVG icon record:
  https://thesvg.org/icon/grok-xai
- CDN source used for the exact SVG bytes:
  https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/glincker/thesvg@main/public/icons/grok-xai/default.svg
- TheSVG lists the icon as MIT and points implementers back to the Grok/xAI
  brand source. xAI's own usage rules are at
  https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines; xAI's official package is
  https://data.x.ai/logos/xAI_Grok_Assets.zip. The package endpoint returned
  HTTP 403 from this environment, so the maintained SVG mirror was used.
- The original `currentColor` fill is explicitly resolved to black for light
  surfaces and white for dark surfaces. Paths and viewBox are unchanged.
- The SVG is a 24×24 viewBox with one path and no external references,
  scripts, or embedded images.

## Gemini

- `gemini.svg` is the current four-colour Google Gemini sparkle, downloaded
  from Wikimedia Commons' copy of Google's published 2025 icon:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Gemini_icon_2025.svg
- Direct source used:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Google%20Gemini%20icon%202025.svg
- The Commons record identifies Google LLC as the author/source and records
  the source as Google's Gemini icon. Google's current brand direction also
  describes the four-colour Gemini spark in
  https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/company-announcements/gradient-g-logo-design/
- The SVG is a 65×65 viewBox and contains only local paths, masks, gradients,
  and filters; no scripts, external URLs, or embedded raster images.
